Academic writing sample task - 1A 

The bar chart shows the number of men and women in Britain enrolled in further education in three periods ten years apart. The chart also depicts the split between people enrolled in full-time education and part-time education.
The number of men enrolled in part-time education peaked at 1 million in 1970-1971. That headcount dipped in the 1980-1981 period to around 800 000 and came back to around 900 000 in 1990-1991. The number of men enrolled in full-time education is much smaller at less than 300 000 in each of the three periods. However, it has steadily increased from around 100 000 in 1970-1971 to more than double that in 1990-1991.
The number of women enrolled in part-time education has seen significant growth from 1970-1971 and 1980-1981 where less than 800 000 women were enrolled to more than 1.1 million in 1990-1991. Despite the growth of females in full-time education from less than 100 000 to more than 200 000 from 1970-1971 to 1980-1981, the headcount has barely increased in the 1990-1991 period.
Overall, women enrolled in part-time further education have surpassed men in the 1990-1991 period. In full-time further education both genders grow in number of enrolled people throughout the three periods shown on the chart.
