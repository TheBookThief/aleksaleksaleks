Better attorneys can facilitate conflict resolution and professionals in medicine can ensure you receive healthcare which is specially tailored for you. However, a person who grew in a family with less money, may be unable to enjoy such connections. They would have to rely on publicly available healthcare and would have to search for a good lawyer through trial and error.
